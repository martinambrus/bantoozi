Privacy

Bantoozi is an invite-only feed reader. This page says what we keep, who processes it and for how long. It is a static page: it runs no scripts and loads nothing from third parties.

What we keep

Your email address, the feeds you subscribe to, your interest cards, labels and rules, and what you do in the reader (read, rate, bookmark, hide). That is the personal data the service works with.

Who processes your data

  • TypeSafe Jev (hosted inference) receives article text for classification. Article text is public content published by the feed owners. Your card texts and the example titles you give are personal; they are not sent to a model provider except as described in the next point.
  • Ollama Cloud is a fallback provider and is off by default. If the operator turns it on, it receives the same kind of requests as Jev.
  • LibreTranslate runs on our own server and translates article text there. Nothing is sent to an outside translation service.

Remote images

Images from publishers are blocked by default. If you allow them for a feed, your browser loads them directly from the publisher, which discloses your IP address and request metadata to that publisher. We keep your per-feed choice and use no-referrer and lazy loading for those images. This page and the sign-in page load no third-party media.

Cookies

Only one: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep things

Bookmarked article snapshots While the article is bookmarked.
Feedback events (ratings, reads) Up to 365 days.
Model call audit 180 days.
Analysis requests 180 days.
Unused invites 30 days after they expire. Used invites are kept for invite accounting while either account exists.
Waitlist entries Deleted when you sign up; otherwise 365 days after you joined.
Sign-in codes 1 day after they expire.
Sessions 30 days after they expire or are revoked.

Deleting your account

When you delete your account, you are signed out everywhere at once and your data stops being used. There is a 7-day grace period: sign in again within 7 days and confirm to undo the deletion. After that your personal data is hard-deleted.

Backups

We keep encrypted daily, weekly and monthly backups. Deleted accounts normally age out of historic backups within 6 months. The one exception is the most recent backup we have verified by a test restore: we keep it until a newer backup passes that test, so if backups or restore tests ever stop, that one backup can be older than 6 months. Backups are never served directly, and if a backup is ever restored, every deletion made since it was taken is replayed before anyone can sign in.

Export

You can download your data at any time from settings (JSON, including your subscriptions, cards, labels, rules, ratings and bookmarks).
