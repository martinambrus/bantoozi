A year of the harbour in ninety seconds

For twelve months a camera on the roof of the harbour master's office took one picture every ten minutes. Put together, the fifty thousand frames show the harbour breathing with the tides and the seasons, from the winter storms to the regatta in August.

The idea came from Tomasz Nowak, a retired pilot who has watched the harbour from his window for thirty years. "People only ever see one moment of it," he said. "I wanted them to see how it moves." The harbour authority paid for the camera and a small solar panel to power it.

Nowak spent most of the summer sorting the pictures, removing the ones spoiled by rain on the lens and the nights when the camera froze. The result will be shown on the big screen at the ferry terminal until the end of the year.
