The market hall reopens under a glass roof

By Marta Král · September 26, 2026

The city's market hall reopened on Friday after two years of renovation, with a new glass roof, a heated floor and room for sixty stalls. Hundreds of people queued outside before the doors opened at seven, and several traders sold out of bread and pastries before nine.

The renovation cost 14 million euros, a third more than planned, after engineers found that the iron columns holding up the roof were badly corroded. Every column was taken down, cleaned and repaired in a workshop outside the city before being put back in its original place.

Traders who had spent the two years in a temporary tent on the square said the new hall was worth the wait. "In the tent we froze in winter and melted in summer," said Pavol Horák, who has sold vegetables at the market for thirty-one years. "Now my customers stay and talk again."

The hall is open from Tuesday to Saturday. On Sundays it will host a flea market and, from November, a Christmas market with food from the whole region. The city will review the rents after the first year, once it knows how many visitors the hall attracts.
