Chipmaker plans second plant as demand for sensors grows

By Daniel Havel · September 24, 2026

The sensor manufacturer Northlight Semiconductors plans to build a second plant on the edge of the harbour district, the company said on Tuesday, as orders from car makers and medical device companies continue to outpace its current capacity.

The new plant, which the company expects to employ about 1,200 people by 2029, would double its production of the image sensors used in driver-assistance systems.
